# Reference material

These files are copied in so the planning docs can point at the original wording. They are not generated.

- `time-mode-strategy-rules.md` is Miguel's time@mode notes (trend and modes, expiry, ATR and expansion confirmations, timeframe in control, the 20-bar rule, 5x jumps, trading blocks, the 70 SMA filter, exits, and the open questions).
- `chart-analysis.md` is the study of the 21 annotated TradingView snapshots, including the clarifications that came out of them (flat modes at a wick, SMA not EMA, ATR(14) with RMA, and the cases that do not match a strict reading).

The runnable scanner that encodes those notes is `prototype/`. Where the notes, the charts, and the scanner disagree, `docs/strategy-spec.md` says which one wins until Miguel answers the open question.
